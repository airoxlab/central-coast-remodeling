What we do

Rooms that work better than they did.

A B-2 licence covers residential remodeling as a whole — so one contractor handles the trades a kitchen or bathroom actually needs, rather than you coordinating four.

Kitchen remodels

Cabinets, countertops, islands, lighting and the layout changes that make a kitchen usable again.

Bathroom remodels

Full bathroom rebuilds — tile, vanities, showers and tubs, waterproofed properly underneath.

Flooring & tile

Hardwood, LVP, tile and stone laid on a prepared subfloor so it sits flat and stays that way.

Cabinets & countertops

New cabinetry or refacing, with countertops templated and fitted to the run.

Drywall & paint

Walls patched, skimmed and finished — the part that decides whether the rest looks right.

Additions & repairs

Room additions, conversions, and the smaller repairs most contractors will not take on.

You deal with the licence holder

California requires a contractor's licence for any job over $500 in labour and materials. Remodeling is where unlicensed work causes the most expensive problems — permits refused, work that fails inspection, and damage that a homeowner's insurance will not cover.

  • Active B-2 licence — the classification specific to residential remodeling.
  • $25,000 contractor's bond with Merchants Bonding Company, on file with the state.
  • Owner-operated — the person who holds the licence is the person doing the work.
  • Free written estimates — the scope and the price in writing before work starts.
